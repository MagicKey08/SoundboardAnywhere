# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-25)

First release.

- Play any soundboard sound through your microphone, without permissions or Nitro
- Web / Vesktop: sounds are mixed straight into the WebRTC mic stream
- Desktop app: sounds are routed through a virtual audio device (Wave Link, VB-CABLE, Voicemeeter)
- Adjustable mic and sound volume, optional self-monitoring
